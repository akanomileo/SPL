# Laptop photos

## Add and edit photos

1. Sign in with your owner Google account at `/owner/` and open **Products**.
2. Add a laptop or click **Edit** on an existing listing.
3. In **Photos**, select several JPG, PNG or WebP images. Each can be up to 3 MB, with up to 12 photos on one laptop. Uploads are checked for supported image structure and dimensions.
4. Check the previews. The first photo is the cover used on the shop’s product card and in the bag. Click **Make cover** on another photo to move it to the front. Use the left/right buttons to change the order; use the X button to remove a photo.
5. Click **Save product** to publish the gallery. A new laptop needs at least one photo. Until you save, changes remain in the editor; **Cancel edit** keeps the previously saved listing.

If one upload fails, photos already uploaded stay in the editor. Choose only the remaining photos to retry, then save. Keep the editor open while uploads finish. Unsaved photo changes do not survive closing or reloading the page.

Use photos of that actual laptop: the screen, keyboard, ports, underside and any wear. New listings start with no sample cover, so upload your own photos before publishing them. Existing listings retain their original cover after upgrading.

## Customer gallery

A product card shows the cover and a photo count when more photos are available. Open **View details** to select thumbnails, use next/previous buttons, or press left/right arrow keys while a gallery control is focused. Click the main photo to open the full-size image in a new tab. Browsing photos requires no Google account. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

Photo changes appear when the customer catalogue refreshes or the customer reloads the shop. The first photo is used consistently as the cover.

## Files and backups

Laptop photos are public catalogue images. Only the authorised owner can upload images or change listings; customer payment receipts remain private and separate.

Removing a photo from a listing does not delete its uploaded file, because other listings may use it. Hidden listings are removed from the catalogue but their image URLs can remain accessible. Upload only images you intend to make public. Back up the entire private data folder, which includes public product/QR uploads and the database, before upgrading. Never place customer receipts in the product gallery.
